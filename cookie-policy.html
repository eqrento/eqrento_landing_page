You can view every page of the Website, including offers prepared for our clients, without agreeing to analytics or marketing.

This Cookie Policy applies to eqrento.com (the “Website”), operated by SOLTIX Sp. z o.o. (“we”, “us”). How we process personal data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device (computer, tablet or phone) and reads on later visits. Similar technologies work in a comparable way: local storage keeps data in your browser, and pixels or tags are small pieces of code that send information about your visit to a server.

These technologies may be first-party (used by the website you visit) or third-party (used by another company whose service the website relies on). Session items disappear when you close the browser; persistent ones stay until they expire or you delete them.

In this Policy, “cookies” covers all of these technologies.

3. Legal basis

Storing information on your device, or reading information already stored there, is governed by Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej), which implements Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC.

  • Necessary items need no consent, because they are required to provide the Website you are using (Article 399(3) of that law). The eq-cookie-consent item falls into this category.
  • Analytics and marketing items may be used only after we have informed you and you have given your consent (Article 399(1)). Where they involve personal data, we process it on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

You can refuse or withdraw consent at any time. Refusing does not limit your access to any part of the Website, including offer pages prepared for our clients.

4. Managing your choices

When you first visit the Website, a banner lets you choose:

  • Accept all – allows the Analytics and Marketing categories;
  • Reject all – keeps only the Necessary category;
  • Customize – lets you switch individual categories on or off and confirm with Save my choices.

Analytics covers Google Analytics 4, Hotjar and Microsoft Clarity. Marketing covers the Google Ads conversion linker and the advertising-related Microsoft cookies used by Clarity. Google Tag Manager loads these tools only after you agree to Analytics; if you agree to Analytics but not to Marketing, the marketing items are not stored.

Until you make a choice, only necessary items are used, and we do not treat continued browsing as consent. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via in the Website footer. When you withdraw consent, we delete the first-party items of these tools from your browser and reload the page, so that they stop working; cookies set on Microsoft’s domains can be deleted in your browser settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

Offer pages that we prepare for individual clients ask only about analytics (Google Analytics, Hotjar and Microsoft Clarity), in a short notice shown after you open the offer; you can change your answer via “Ustawienia cookies” at the bottom of the offer page. It is the same setting as the Analytics category in the banner, so your choice applies to the whole Website; your Marketing choice stays as you set it in the banner.

You can also decide in your browser settings whether websites may store and read information on your device, and delete what is already stored:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari on Mac; on iPhone and iPad, in the device Settings under Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you block or clear local storage, the Website cannot remember your choice and will show the banner again.

5. Analytics and marketing tools

Google Tag Manager. We load the tools below with Google Tag Manager, a service of Google Ireland Limited, and only after you agree to Analytics.

Google Analytics 4. We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, to count visits and see how the Website is used: which pages are viewed and for how long, on what kind of device and browser, from which country or city, and from which page or campaign visitors arrive. On offer pages prepared for individual clients, it also records whether the offer has been opened and which of its parts were viewed, for example the price, so that we know whether our offer has been read. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses; it uses them only to determine the approximate location.

Google Analytics runs only if you agree to the Analytics category: it is loaded, and its cookies are set, only after your consent. We use the results as statistics, to improve the Website and our offers.

Google processes this data for us under its data processing terms. Data about individual visits is kept in Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months; after that, only aggregated statistics remain. Google may process the data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, on the basis of the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (see section 6 of our Privacy Policy). Google explains how it uses information from websites that use its services. You can also block Google Analytics on all websites with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Hotjar. Hotjar, provided by Hotjar Limited (Malta, company number C65490; part of the Contentsquare group), records how visitors use the pages – clicks, taps, mouse movements and scrolling, together with the page content as it is shown, including offer pages prepared for individual clients – and shows them to us as recordings and heatmaps. Text you type into form fields is not recorded. Hotjar also collects technical data (type of device, screen size, browser), the country and a random user ID; it does not store IP addresses. The data is stored in Ireland (EU) on Amazon Web Services; some of Hotjar’s sub-processors are outside the EU. Recordings and heatmaps are kept for 365 days. According to Hotjar, it may also reuse this data to develop and improve its tools and services.

Microsoft Clarity. Microsoft Clarity, provided by Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, Ireland, records visits in the same way – mouse movements, clicks, scrolling and how the page is displayed, including offer pages – and creates heatmaps; content entered in form fields is masked and not sent. Microsoft acts as a controller of this data. It is stored in the Microsoft Azure cloud and may be transferred to Microsoft Corporation in the United States, which is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; Microsoft Ireland also has standard contractual clauses with it. We can view recordings for up to 30 days and heatmaps for up to 9 months. We pass your choice on to Clarity: without your consent to Analytics it sets no cookies, and its advertising-related cookies are allowed only if you also agree to Marketing. More in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Google Ads conversion linker (Marketing). If you agree to Marketing, this Google tool stores information about the Google ad you clicked to reach the Website, so that we can see whether our ads lead to visits and demo requests. Without your consent to Marketing, it stores nothing. It is provided by Google Ireland Limited, and the data may be processed in the United States on the basis of the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Before we start using any other analytics or marketing tool, we will list it in the table in section 2, update the date of this Policy and ask for your choice again.
