FAQ

Questions rental companies ask

Do I need the full EQrento system to use GPS?

No. The GPS module works on its own, with reports and overuse detection. To check overuse, EQrento needs the rental period of each machine: enter it in EQrento or import it from Excel. The rest of the platform is there when you want it.

How are engine hours counted?

The tracker is connected to the ignition. Every period when the machine is switched on is logged with start time, end time and location, and added up as engine hours.

Who installs the device?

Usually your own mechanic, with our step-by-step guide. The tracker needs power, ground and the ignition signal.

Will overuse be charged automatically?

No. EQrento flags it and you decide. Only approved items go on the invoice, together with a PDF report your customer can check.

We already pay for telematics. Do we have to switch?

No. If you use Trackunit or Zoomlion telematics, EQrento can read that data. Many companies combine both: existing telematics on new machines, our tracker on the rest.

What happens to our data if we stop?

The data is yours. You can export it at any time.
