The problem

Your machines work more than you invoice

Nobody is cheating. The customer has a deadline, the machine is on site, and nobody compares the hour meter with the contract.

  • Weekends and holidays

    Rented Monday to Friday, working on Saturday. The invoice still says five days.

  • Double shifts

    The contract says 8 hours a day. The machine runs 14. The hour meter knows, the invoice does not.

  • Night work

    The contract covers daytime work. The machine also runs at night, and nobody adds it to the invoice.

How it works

From GPS to invoice in six steps

The same flow that runs in the full EQrento platform, available as a standalone GPS module.

  1. 01TrackerTeltonika device on the machine: position, ignition and battery voltage.
  2. 02Engine hoursEvery ignition-on period is logged with start, end and location.
  3. 03Contract checkHours are compared with the rental period, working days and daily limit.
  4. 04Overuse flaggedWeekends, holidays, night work and overtime land in one report.
  5. 05Your approvalYou decide what to charge. Nothing goes to the customer without you.
  6. 06InvoiceThe extra line goes on the invoice with a PDF report as proof.

The device

Three inputs.
All you need for billing.

A proven Teltonika tracker, connected to the machine’s ignition and battery. Your mechanic can install it with our step-by-step guide.

  • Ignition: engine hours

    The yellow wire goes to the ignition. Every minute the machine is switched on counts towards its engine hours.

  • Battery voltage

    See the battery condition of every machine in the report, useful for electric scissor lifts and machines standing idle on site.

  • GPS position

    Where every machine is right now and where it worked. Find machines faster at collection.
