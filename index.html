Key features
and competitive differentiators

Build an offer in under a minute from live fleet availability – machines are picked and priced for you, with hourly, daily and weekend rates, discounts and delivery by distance. One click turns it into a contract, signed electronically on mobile.

Your whole fleet at a glance: what’s out, what’s back and what’s ready for the next job. Every unit has a drag-and-drop calendar, its documents and full history – plus a QR code that opens its file on site. You get automatic reminders when an inspection is due or a document needs updating.

Deliveries and pickups are created automatically once a contract is signed or paid, planned in the transport calendar and sent to the driver’s app. Handovers are recorded on site with photos and a signature – nothing gets lost between the office and the yard.

Every machine on a live map, with engine hours checked against the contract. Work outside agreed hours, at weekends or outside the permitted area is detected and added to the invoice automatically, with a PDF report as proof. Works with your telematics or our GPS device.

Every return creates a checklist, so no machine goes out unchecked. Maintenance is scheduled with reminders, repairs are assigned to technicians and logged in the mechanic app with photos and signatures – and downtime shows before it blocks a booking.

When a rental ends, the invoice is ready – calculated from the contract and the data collected on the way: rental time, delivery, fuel, cleaning and overtime from telematics. It is sent automatically to the customer and to your accounting or ERP system, with payment reminders to follow.

Every machine tracked. Every hour billed.

Telematics that safeguards your billing
