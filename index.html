Key features
and competitive differentiators

Build an offer in under a minute from live fleet availability – machines are picked and priced for you from day-range and monthly price lists, with discounts and any additional costs you need. One click turns it into a contract, signed electronically on mobile.

Your whole fleet at a glance: what’s out, what’s back and what’s ready for the next job. Every unit has a drag-and-drop calendar, its documents and full history – plus a QR code that opens its file on site. You get automatic reminders when an inspection is due or a document needs updating.

Deliveries and pickups are created automatically once a contract is signed or paid, planned in the transport calendar and sent to the driver’s app. Handovers are recorded on site with photos and a signature – nothing gets lost between the office and the yard.

Every machine on a live map, with engine hours checked against the contract. Work outside agreed hours, at weekends or outside the permitted area is detected and added to the invoice automatically, with a PDF report as proof. Works with your telematics or our GPS device.

Every return creates a checklist, so no machine goes out unchecked. Maintenance is scheduled with reminders, repairs are assigned to technicians and logged in the mechanic app with photos and signatures – and downtime shows before it blocks a booking.

Spare parts and products in every warehouse, with stock levels and value always up to date. Stock coming in, going out and moving between warehouses is recorded as it happens, parts taken for a service order are booked to that repair, and you’re alerted when an item falls below its minimum level. Stocktaking keeps the numbers right, with opening and closing balances for your books.

Sell machines from your fleet and spare parts in the same system you rent with. Sales offers, contracts and invoices are handled there too, and a machine out on rent can be sold straight to its renter. Every sold machine keeps its buyer, serial number, documents and warranty, and after-sales service carries on – service orders and inspection reminders, just like for your own fleet.

When a rental ends, the invoice is ready – calculated automatically from the contract and the data collected on the way (rental time, delivery, fuel, cleaning and overtime from telematics), and still yours to adjust. It goes to the customer and to your accounting or ERP system, and unpaid invoices are chased automatically with reminder emails and PDFs.

Ask about your business in plain language: which machines sat idle, who owes you money, how much each branch earned. The AI Assistant reads all your EQrento data – equipment, orders, transport, service, telematics and invoices – and answers in seconds with a table or a chart, showing where the numbers came from. No reports to build or browse.
