No machine goes out unchecked.

From the Complete plan

What it does for you

  • A checklist after every return

    A returned machine is checked before it goes out again and marked as efficient, small repair or major repair – so nothing leaves the yard unchecked.

  • Recurring service that schedules itself

    Set one rule per product group – every so many engine hours or days – and service tasks are created automatically, with reminders before the due date.

  • Repairs logged next to the machine

    Mechanics get their tasks in the mobile app and close them with a protocol: photos, the parts and services used, and a signature.

  • From protocol to service invoice

    An approved protocol becomes a service invoice, with several protocols on one invoice when you need it. Service costs land in the machine’s history and profitability.

All features

Everything in Service, from the Complete plan. A tag marks a feature that starts with a higher plan.

Checks and recurring service

  • Checklists after a machine’s return, with statuses: to check, efficient, small repair, major repair
  • Recurring service: one rule per product group (every X engine hours or every X days) creates service tasks automatically
  • Reminders about inspections and due dates
  • The upcoming service shown on the equipment list
  • Maintenance log available under the machine’s QR code
  • Downtime visible before a booking is accepted

Repairs and the mechanic app

  • Repairs with statuses: new, pending, in progress, to be approved, done, incomplete service
  • Fault reports from the contract and from the app, with a notification for the service team
  • Service calendar split by mechanic
  • Mobile app for mechanics (Android): tasks and protocols with photos and a signature
  • Several protocols for one repair, with parts and services listed on each
  • Protocol approval by a coordinator or supervisor Connected
  • Service of external equipment (your customers’ machines)

Costs and invoices

  • Service invoice created from an approved protocol
  • Several protocols on one invoice
  • Service costs in the machine’s history and profitability

Works with the rest of EQrento

Equipment Every check, repair and cost is kept in the machine’s history. Warehouse (WMS) Parts are reserved for service orders and issued to them. GPS & Telematics Engine hours from GPS keep recurring service on schedule.

Plans

Part of the Complete plan

Checklists, the service calendar, repair protocols and the mechanic app come with Complete. Service reminders, fault reporting and the repair history are already in Essential, and supervisor approval starts with Connected.
