Key features · Warehouse (WMS)

Parts and stock under control.
Tied to every service order.

Stock levels and value across all your warehouses, always up to date. Parts taken for a repair are booked to that service order, and you’re alerted when an item runs low.

Book a 30-minute demo See all features

In the Enterprise plan

What it does for you

  • Every warehouse in one picture

    Stock levels and their value for each warehouse, kept in order with inventory categories – and an alert when an item falls below its minimum level.

  • Parts follow the repair

    Parts are reserved for a service order and issued to it, so the repair shows its real cost and the shelf shows the real stock.

  • Movements recorded as they happen

    Receipts and issues get their documents automatically, and transfers move stock between warehouses without a spreadsheet.

  • Counts and balances for your books

    Stocktaking comes with a search box and a draft that saves itself, plus opening and closing balances for your books.

All features

Everything in Warehouse (WMS), part of the Enterprise plan.

Stock

  • Multiple warehouses, with stock levels and their value
  • Inventory categories for parts and products
  • Low-stock alerts when an item falls below its minimum level

Stock movements

  • Movement documents created automatically for receipts and issues
  • Transfers between warehouses
  • Parts reserved for service orders and issued to them
  • External issues and the sale of products with an invoice (the issue is created automatically)

Stocktaking and records

  • Stocktaking with a search box and automatic saving of the draft
  • Opening and closing balances
  • Purchase history and purchase values of parts and products

Works with the rest of EQrento

Service Parts are reserved for service orders and issued to them. Equipment sales Spare parts and products are sold straight from stock. Invoicing A sale from stock creates its invoice and its issue.

Plans

Part of the Enterprise plan

Warehouse (WMS) comes with Enterprise, together with equipment and parts sales, custom integrations and a dedicated account manager. Enterprise is priced for your fleet.
